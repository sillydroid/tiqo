❓ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No! At Tiko, you never need to create an account, remember passwords, or verify emails. Just select your tickets, enter your name and phone, and pay with M-Pesa.

How does the Referral Program work without an account?

No account or registration is required! Your unique referral code is permanently tied to the M-Pesa phone number and email you used when booking your ticket. When friends use your referral code at checkout, our system identifies your initial booking reference and credits the reward directly to your M-Pesa phone number or applies it as an automatic discount on your next booking.

Note: The reward and discount amounts (such as KSh 100) are illustrative and may vary from time to time based on active promotional campaigns, ticket tier, or event organizer settings.

How do I receive my ticket?

Immediately after your M-Pesa PIN is entered, your booking is confirmed in real-time. You receive a digital ticket with a gate QR code on your screen and can download a high-res PDF ticket instantly.

Can I buy tickets for an event that has already ended?

No. Once an event date has passed, ticket sales automatically lock and close. Past events remain searchable for reference, but new ticket purchases are strictly disabled.
